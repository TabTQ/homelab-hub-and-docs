Month view. Sample people (Anu, Ravi, Meera) and sample amounts, only to show the layout. Every figure on this screen was checked to add up.
  • Pick any month with the arrows or the month picker. Nothing is locked: every month stays editable, and people can be added at any time.
  • Who pays whom already includes what was carried in from earlier unpaid months (here ₹300.00 that Ravi owed Meera from August). Balances are netted across everyone, so fewer payments are needed.
  • Mark paid records a settlement (with a date). The transfer then shows as paid, and it stops being carried into later months.
  • An entry with no day shows just the month and counts as the 15th (see Electricity bill). The “recurring” badge marks an entry created from a recurring expense.

Add expense. One entry at a time, opened from the “+ Add expense” button. On a phone it slides up as a sheet; on desktop it is a dialog.
  • Only the month is required. Leave Day empty and the entry is stored as the 15th and shown as “Sep (no day)”. Choose a day if you know it. (Try the toggle.)
  • Split among lists your saved people, all ticked to start. Untick anyone who is not part of it. + Add person works right here, at any time, without leaving the form (try it).
  • The grey line shows the exact split before you save, including the leftover paisa.
  • Only the payment name is needed. There are no categories.

Checks that stop a bad entry
  • Amount must be more than zero, with at most 2 decimals.
  • At least one person must be in the split. The payer may or may not be in it.
  • Name required (up to 100 characters).
  • A new person is saved to your People list as well, and appears in future forms.
  • Editing an earlier expense lets you add the new person to it. Older expenses are not changed automatically.
Recurring expenses. Set once; each month gets its entry automatically.
  • Monthly only. Starts is a month, Ends is an optional month. Day is optional (blank = the 15th; the 31st becomes the last day of shorter months).
  • When you open a month, its recurring entries are created once, as ordinary entries you can edit or delete for that month only. A deleted one is not created again.
  • Editing the recurring item changes months you have not opened yet. Months already opened keep their entries, so old summaries never change.
  • Nothing runs in the background: the entries appear when you open the month.

People. Set up once; they appear in “Paid by” and “Split among”.
  • Add a person at any time, including in the middle of a month. Nothing is locked.
  • A person with history cannot be deleted (their old entries would break). Archive hides them from new forms but keeps every past entry and balance. Restore brings them back.
  • Renaming updates the name everywhere, including old entries.
  • An archived person with an unsettled balance is still shown in the summary until it is settled.

Export a PDF. Choose this month, or any range, then share it from your phone.
  • This month gives exactly what the month screen shows, including balances carried in.
  • Custom range: carried in is worked out properly as the balance just before the range starts (all earlier expenses minus all settlements made before that date). Expenses and settlements inside the range are then listed and added on top, and “who pays whom” is the net result. So the PDF for any range is a correct statement on its own.
  • Expenses with no day count as the 15th; the PDF marks them “no day”.
  • Share opens the phone's share sheet (WhatsApp, email, Drive). If the browser can't share files, it downloads instead. The file is named like budget-2026-09.pdf.
